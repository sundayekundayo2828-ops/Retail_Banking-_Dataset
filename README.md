Retail Banking Portfolio Analysis
Executive Overview
This project analyzes a 10,000‑row retail banking customer portfolio to identify systemic revenue risks and uncover immediate growth opportunities. The assessment covers:

Customer retention & churn risk

Cross‑selling potential

Digital engagement behavior

Credit risk segmentation

Wealth tier distribution

The goal is to transform raw banking data into actionable business intelligence for leadership and operational teams.
